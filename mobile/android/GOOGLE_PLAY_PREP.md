## Future Ads Path

Recommended monetization path:

1. Publish app safely without ads first.
2. Create Google AdMob account.
3. Add Rewarded Ads to the Android app.
4. Free users watch rewarded ads to unlock signals.
5. Paid users stay ad-free.

Do not add advertising claims until AdMob approval is granted.
