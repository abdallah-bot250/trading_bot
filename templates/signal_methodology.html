Signal methodology

Signals are filtered, not guessed.

Nexora scans liquid crypto markets and waits for structured setups. A signal is considered only when trend, support and resistance, momentum, volume, and risk/reward agree enough to justify an alert.

1

Trend Direction

The engine checks EMA structure and recent price action so it does not buy blindly into weak downtrends or short blindly into strong rebounds.

2

Support & Resistance

Recent swing highs and lows are clustered into levels. Stronger levels need repeated touches and recent reactions before they influence targets.

3

Momentum

RSI and candle behavior help confirm whether price is reacting from a level or already exhausted after a late move.

4

Volume Confirmation

Signals are stronger when the move has enough market participation. Weak or thin pairs are filtered before scanning.

5

Risk / Reward

Targets are based around the next meaningful support or resistance area. Weak setups with poor upside are skipped.

6

No Duplicate Noise

The bot avoids repeating the same pair too often and logs why a signal passed or was skipped.
