# Nexora AI Trader Brand Guide

## Brand Name

Primary name: **Nexora AI Trader**

Short name: **Nexora**

Product category: AI-powered crypto signal and trading assistant platform.

## Positioning

Nexora AI Trader is a premium trading intelligence platform for users who want cleaner crypto signals, safer decision workflows, Telegram delivery, and optional Elite automation.

## Company Identity

Company voice:
- Confident, precise, and protective.
- Premium without exaggerated profit promises.
- Clear about trading risk.
- Focused on quality, filtering, and user control.

Core promise:
Nexora helps reduce random trading decisions by combining market analysis, AI-style filtering, Telegram delivery, and account controls in one premium experience.

## Logo

Primary logo:
`/static/brand/nexora-logo.svg`

Favicon:
`/static/brand/favicon.svg`

Logo usage:
- Use the full logo on landing pages, dashboards, documents, and pitch material.
- Use the favicon/mark in browser tabs, app icons, small cards, and compact headers.
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the N mark.
- Do not stretch, recolor, rotate, or add shadows outside the approved SVG.

## Typography

Primary font stack:
`Inter, Segoe UI, Tahoma, Arial, sans-serif`

Arabic/UI fallback:
`Cairo, Segoe UI, Tahoma, Arial, sans-serif`

Usage:
- Large headlines: 700-900 weight, tight line-height.
- Interface labels/buttons: 700-800 weight.
- Body copy: 400-500 weight, relaxed line-height.
- Never use negative letter spacing.

## UI Style

Theme:
- Premium dark.
- Glass panels with 8px radius.
- Restrained gradients.
- Clear button hierarchy.
- Light motion only.

Primary CTA:
- Green gradient using `#20D68A` to `#10B981`.
- Dark text on CTA for contrast.

Secondary CTA:
- Dark translucent surface with light text.

## Messaging

Approved phrases:
- "AI-powered crypto signal platform"
- "Cleaner signals, fewer random entries"
- "Quality filters before every signal"
- "Telegram delivery with account controls"
- "Elite automation is optional"

Avoid:
- Guaranteed profit claims.
- "Risk free"
- "100% win rate"
- Unrealistic income promises.

Required risk note:
Trading contains risk. Nexora AI Trader helps organize decisions and reduce randomness, but it does not guarantee profit.
